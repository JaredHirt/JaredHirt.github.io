How To Play

  • 💰 Goal: Grow your Net Worth. You start with $5,000.
  • ⏰ Market: Prices update once per hour.
  • 💸 Dividends: Stocks priced over $1.00 pay dividends every hour. Cheap stocks pay nothing!
  • 📉 The Crash: If a stock hits $0, it crashes. You lose your investment.
  • 📈 The Split: If a stock hits $2.00, it splits. You get 2x shares, price resets to $1.00.
  • 💀 Bankruptcy: Lost it all? You can declare bankruptcy to reset to $2,000 (72h cooldown).
